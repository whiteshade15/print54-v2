A shop sign in Brussels: check the permit

In the Brussels-Capital Region, putting up a shop sign generally requires a planning permit. The rules depend on the zone your business is in and on the type of sign (flat on the facade, projecting, illuminated).

Your local council's planning department can tell you whether your project needs one. Applications go through the Region's MyPermit platform.

Mention it when you request a quote: the size and position of the sign depend on it.

Sources: be.brussels (Regional Urban Planning Regulation, title VI).
