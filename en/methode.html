How we work: from photo to installation

Four steps, always in the same order. You approve before anything is spent.

  1. 01

    Your request

    You describe the project: photos, measurements, logo, deadline.

    A photo of the shopfront or vehicle is enough to start.

  2. 02

    The mock-up

    Print54 prepares a mock-up. You ask for changes or approve it.

    The price is set on the approved mock-up.

  3. 03

    Production

    The approved project is printed and made.

    Lead time depends on the type of sign and the quantities.

  4. 04

    Installation

    Print54 installs the sign, lettering or film on site.

    Installation is scheduled with you, depending on access to the site.

What helps for a quick quote

  • A straight-on photo of the shopfront, window or vehicle
  • Measurements, even rough ones
  • Your logo, ideally as PDF, AI or SVG
  • The text to display
  • Your target date
